## Icons

`wwwroot/js/lucide.min.js` is Lucide 0.468.0, downloaded from
https://unpkg.com/lucide@0.468.0/dist/umd/lucide.min.js.
The vendored bundle retains its copyright and license notices.
The full license is included in `wwwroot/js/LICENSE-lucide.txt`.
Lucide uses the [ISC license](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/0.468.0/LICENSE),
including the MIT notice for inherited Feather icons.

## Fonts and Map

Playfair Display and DM Sans are requested from Google Fonts, with local serif
and sans-serif fallbacks. Their distributions use the SIL Open Font License.
The Find Us page embeds Google Maps for an explicitly illustrative address.
